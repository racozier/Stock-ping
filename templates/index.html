How It Works
  1. Add a stock ticker to your watchlist.
  2. Click a row to view a live chart.
  3. Click Set Alert to choose price, % change, or technical alerts.
  4. Stock-Ping checks prices every 60 seconds.
  5. When your target is hit you'll hear a sound, see a notification, and get a push on your phone.
Phone setup: Install the free ntfy app, tap +, and subscribe to your topic (configure in Settings).
