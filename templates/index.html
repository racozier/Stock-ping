How It Works
  1. Add a stock ticker to your watchlist.
  2. Click Set Alert and choose a target price and direction.
  3. Stock-Ping checks prices every 60 seconds.
  4. When your target is hit you'll hear a sound, see a Windows notification, and get a push on your phone.
Phone setup: Install the free ntfy app on Android, tap +, and subscribe to your topic (configure in Settings).
